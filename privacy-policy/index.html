1. Introduction

At Aptivance, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle the information you provide when you use our website, explore our courses and internships, or contact us through our enquiry forms.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, educational details, course or internship interests, and any information you choose to include in your message.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, provide information about our courses and internship opportunities, communicate with you through available channels such as WhatsApp, and improve our website and services.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the information you provide with trusted team members, service providers, or partners when it is necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide the requested course or internship information.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the website and to improve the overall user experience. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide to us. However, no method of storing or transferring information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us if you want to update, correct, or request the removal of personal information that you have provided through our website.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website or services. Any updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at hello@aptivance.com .

Your trust matters to us. We use your information responsibly to help you explore courses, internships, and learning opportunities with Aptivance.
